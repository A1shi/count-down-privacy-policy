Privacy Policy for Count Down

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Count Down ("the app") is a countdown and day-counting application developed by Aashi Gupta. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information Collection and Use

The current version of Count Down does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Local Data

Information created by users within the app, such as countdown names and dates, is stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of providing the app's functionality. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Notifications and Widgets

Count Down may use device notifications and home-screen widgets to display countdown information and reminders. These features operate on the user's device and do not require the collection of personal data by the developer.

Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not use advertising services, analytics services, user accounts, or cloud-based data storage.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if future versions of the app introduce new features or change how information is handled. Any updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, users may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
