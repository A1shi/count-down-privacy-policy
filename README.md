
# Countdown & Widget — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Countdown & Widget** mobile application ("App") handles user information.

Countdown & Widget is a countdown and deadline management application that allows users to create countdowns, display countdown widgets, and receive local notifications.

## 1. Information We Collect

Countdown & Widget is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

### Personal Information

The App does **not** require users to create an account and does not collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Passwords
- Contacts
- Location data
- Financial information

### Countdown Data

Countdowns and deadlines created by the user may be stored locally on the user's device so that the App can display countdowns and widgets correctly.

This may include:

- Countdown title
- Target date and time
- Reminder settings
- Widget configuration

This information is not transmitted to our servers.

## 2. Notifications

The App may request permission to send notifications.

Notifications are used to:

- Remind users about upcoming deadlines
- Notify users when a countdown reaches its configured time

Notifications are generated locally by the App and are not used for advertising.

Users can disable notification permissions through Android device settings.

## 3. Home Screen Widgets

The App provides Android home screen widgets for displaying countdowns and deadlines.

Widget information is generated from data stored on the user's device. The App does not transmit widget data to external servers.

## 4. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions depending on the features being used:

### POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to display countdown and deadline notifications.

### SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Used when the App needs to schedule a notification at a specific user-selected time.

### RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used to restore scheduled countdown notifications after the device is restarted.

The App does not use these permissions to collect personal information.

## 5. Data Storage

Countdown and deadline information is stored locally on the user's device.

The App does not operate a backend server for storing user countdown data.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored application data according to Android's standard behavior.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Data Security

Because user countdown information is stored locally on the device, users should maintain appropriate security on their Android device, including device locks and operating-system security updates.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information, it is designed to minimize privacy risks for all users.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
